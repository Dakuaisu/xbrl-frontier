### notes
Net answer: nothing in Jul-Sep 2026 fine-tunes or distills a 1-3B model on FinTagging, and nothing reports a cost/latency frontier for small models on FinTagging/FNXL/FiNER. The lm2445 datasets are not a scoop-in-progress for this project: they were built by Linhai Ma (The Fin AI) for the Factorized Hypothesis Search paper (arXiv 2608.06614, 6 Aug 2026), a zero-shot Qwen3-32B + BM25 retrieval pipeline; the 2,509-row Grounding set matches the paper's test split exactly, and lm2445 has still uploaded no FinTagging models. The paper's own limitations section says model scale vs cost is open. Two caveats for the novelty wording: FinLoRA (May 2025) already plots average inference time of LoRA-tuned Llama-3.1-8B vs Gemini on FiNER-139/FNXL and gives training cost in dollars; FHS gives LLM-calls-per-fact and wall-clock hours (CodiEsp split only). Neither gives $/query serving cost, quantized variants, or a 1-3B model, and neither touches FinTagging with a fine-tuned small model. Safer phrasing: "no prior work reports a serving-cost/latency frontier for fine-tuned 1-3B models on FinTagging against frontier APIs."
