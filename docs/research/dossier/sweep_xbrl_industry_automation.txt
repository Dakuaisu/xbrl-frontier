### verdict_on_novelty
DENTED, not dead. The literal claim 'prior work reports accuracy but NOT serving cost or latency' is false as stated: (1) FinLoRA (arXiv 2505.19819, VERIFIED) evaluates LoRA-fine-tuned Llama-3.1-8B on FiNER-139, FNXL and its own XBRL tag/value-extraction tasks and reports fine-tuning cost in USD ($14.66-$16.54 on 4xA5000 vs $162.02 Gemini 2.0 FL vs $312 GPT-4o-mini), GPU memory at 16/8/4-bit, and 'Figure 3: Average inference time of LoRA fine-tuned Llama 3.1 8B and LoRA fine-tuned Gemini 2.0 FL across tasks'; (2) FLAN-FinXC (arXiv 2405.06671, VERIFIED) reports 'inference time: 2 minutes/sample' for a 780M FLAN-T5-Large on FNXL/FiNER; (3) FinTagging itself (VERIFIED) already scores Qwen3-0.6B/1.7B and Llama-3.2-3B zero-shot and states 'Total cost is approximately 500 GPU-hours and $1,500 in API usage'; (4) Kulkarni & Kulkarni 2026 (arXiv 2603.22651, VERIFIED) report per-document cost ($0.148-$0.430) and p50 latency (21-74 s) for SEC 10-K/10-Q field extraction with frontier and 70B vLLM-served models. What remains open and defensible: no work found that fine-tunes a 1-3B model, serves it with vLLM/quantization, and reports a per-model accuracy-cost-latency frontier against frontier APIs on FinTagging (or on FNXL/FiNER with per-fact or per-filing serving cost). Industry (Workiva patents '>95% accuracy', DFIN June-2026 AI tagging, IRIS CARBON '95% auto-tagged', CoreFiling Seahorse) publishes no benchmarks, costs, or latencies, so the industry angle poses no threat but offers no citable baseline beyond unsourced consultancy numbers (LP&M: 120h->35h per 10-K; $60k->$15k/yr). Recommended rewording: 'FinLoRA and FLAN-FinXC report training cost or coarse inference time for 8B/780M models on FiNER/FNXL; FinTagging reports only aggregate evaluation cost; no prior work reports a serving cost/latency frontier for fine-tuned sub-4B models on FinTagging.'
