### verdict_on_novelty
From this angle the claim is DENTED, not dead. (1) 'Prior XBRL work reports accuracy but NOT latency' is false as literally stated: FinLoRA v2 (arXiv 2505.19819) reports per-task inference time of LoRA-fine-tuned Llama 3.1 8B vs fine-tuned Gemini 2.0 FL across FiNER-139, FNXL, XBRL Analysis etc. (Figure 3; text: 'Gemini API generally exhibits lower inference latency ... than local Llama 3.1 8B Instruct inference ... the inference speed of locally deployed Llama models can be significantly enhanced through the use of larger batch sizes') and FinLoRA v1 (arXiv 2412.11378) tabulates 'Time per Example (s) 1.9' for 4-bit/8-bit Llama 3.1 8B on XBRL tag/value extraction; FLAN-FinXC (2405.06671) even notes 'inference time: 2 minutes/sample' on FNXL/FiNER. (2) 'NOT serving cost' survives in narrowed form: FinLoRA reports only fine-tuning cost ($14.66-$16.54 on 4xA5000 vs $162 Gemini / $312 GPT-4o-mini), FinTagging only an aggregate budget ('Total cost is approximately 500 GPU-hours and $1,500 in API usage'), and none of FinLoRA, FinTagging, Open FinLLM Leaderboard, XBRL Agent, the COLING 2025 Regulations Challenge, or FinAI Contest 2025 report $/query or $/1k facts, throughput, or a quantized-serving (vLLM) frontier against frontier APIs. (3) 'Nobody has benchmarked small or distilled models on FinTagging/FNXL/FiNER with cost or latency' is partly wrong on the model side and right on the metric side: FinTagging already evaluates Qwen3-0.6B/1.7B and Llama-3.2-3B zero-shot (near-zero scores, no cost/latency); FinAI Contest 2025 Task 2 publishes Llama-3.1-8B 8-bit/4-bit LoRA adapters for FinNI/FinCL (no numbers); the Regulations Challenge fine-tuned Llama 3.2-3B on XBRL QA (accuracy only). No work was found that fine-tunes a 1-3B model on FinTagging and reports accuracy + serving cost + latency; but adjacent 2026 work already does this framing elsewhere (How Small Can You Go? 270M-8B LoRA with throughput on financial transaction extraction, arXiv 2606.08051; cost-per-document and p50 latency on 10,000 SEC filings with vLLM-served open models, arXiv 2603.22651). Recommended restatement: 'FinLoRA reports fine-tuning cost and a per-task inference-time figure for an 8B LoRA model, and FinTagging benchmarks 0.6B-3B models zero-shot, but no prior work reports a serving cost / latency / accuracy frontier for fine-tuned 1-3B (quantized, vLLM-served) models on FinTagging against frontier APIs.'
