### unknowns
(1) The exact identity of the 1,949 audited instances is not recoverable from any public file (no flag; corrections merged back) - only the authors can provide it. (2) The share of auto-labels the annotators judged incorrect is not reported; the ~12% figure is a kappa-derived inference on a deliberately hard subset. (3) No random-sample precision/recall audit of FinTagging labels exists; NORA's '<5% label errors' is on its own 10-K/10-Q corpus with an unstated sample size, and no FiNER/FNXL-specific noise rates were found. (4) 111 of the 142 eval registrants were recovered from 890 FTS queries; a complete manifest needs more queries (~2-3k) or the authors. (5) Whether the authors consider the AT&T/USAC/ACRE/Loews-CNA overlap intentional is unknown; the paper is silent. (6) NORA's 6.6M-instance benchmark has no public release link as of 2026-10-01. (7) WebSearch budget was exhausted at session start, so discovery used arXiv/HF/GitHub/S2/EDGAR APIs via curl rather than web search.

### notes
Bottom line: (a) FinTagging's released training split is NOT company-disjoint from its evaluation split - 4 of 10 training companies (AT&T, USA Compression, Ares Commercial Real Estate as prior-fiscal-year filings; Loews as the same-fiscal-year parent of CNA Financial) are in the eval set, and 78 training instances (35 company-specific, 30 with identical labels) are verbatim eval contexts. A fine-tune on the released train set must drop those companies, or better, build a fresh training corpus from CIKs outside the recovered eval manifest and outside the 2023-02-13..2025-02-13 window. (b) The label-noise ceiling is unquantified by the authors; the usable handles are the kappa-implied ~10-14% flagged-incorrect rate on the audited hard subset, NORA's <5% random-sample tag-error rate on comparable iXBRL-derived labels, and the fact that 99.25% of FinTagging entities are unaudited. A credible write-up should re-audit a random ~300-500 instance sample of FinNI-eval-v2/FinCL-eval-v2 and report a noise-adjusted ceiling, or adopt an NPK-style filtered split. (c) The 142-CIK manifest is recoverable with the recipe used here (exact 11-12-word phrase, forms=10-K, custom date window, keep queries with 1-2 distinct CIKs, collapse co-registrant CIKs); 111/142 were recovered in ~15 minutes of throttled queries.
