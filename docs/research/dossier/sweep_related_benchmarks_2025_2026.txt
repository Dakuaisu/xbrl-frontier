### verdict_on_novelty
DENTED, not dead. The literal claim "nobody has benchmarked small or distilled models on FinTagging/FNXL/FiNER with cost or latency" fails on two points: (1) FinLoRA (arXiv 2505.19819, VERIFIED) fine-tunes Llama 3.1 8B with LoRA/QLoRA on FiNER-139 and FNXL, compares to GPT-4o/DeepSeek-V3/Gemini, reports fine-tuning cost in dollars ("$14.66 to $16.54" on 4xA5000 vs GPT-4o-mini $312) and a per-task inference-time figure (Figure 3, "Inference Time (seconds)", with FiNER and FNXL columns, local Llama-8B vs Gemini API); (2) FinTagging itself (VERIFIED) already places Qwen3-0.6B, Qwen3-1.7B, Llama-3.2-3B and Llama-3.1-8B on its leaderboard (zero-shot, FinNI F1 0.004-0.24, FinCL acc 0.04-0.09) and ships a training set, and FLaME (VERIFIED) reports aggregate inference dollars per model on a suite containing FiNER/FNXL. Separately, the "nobody reports serving cost or latency for SEC fact extraction" sub-claim is contradicted outside these three datasets by arXiv 2603.22651 (cost per document $0.187-$0.430 and p50 latency 38.7-74.1 s for 25-field extraction from 10,000 10-K/10-Q/8-K with XBRL-derived gold labels, frontier + Llama-3-70B/Mixtral via vLLM), the SECB/earnings-call paper 2605.03147 (Table 14: per-model runtime and API cost, Qwen3-30B-A3B $0.023 vs Gemini-3-pro $2.65), and Stanford EDGAR-OCR 2606.18192 (median latency 2.29 s vs 24.58 s). What remains intact and defensible: no found work (a) fine-tunes or distills a 1-3B model on FinTagging (FinNI+FinCL over the full US-GAAP taxonomy) or on FNXL/FiNER at that scale, (b) serves it with quantized vLLM variants, and (c) reports a per-fact accuracy/cost/latency frontier against current frontier APIs; FinLoRA stops at 8B, reports no per-query inference cost, evaluates FNXL on only 247 samples, and does not touch FinTagging; the multi-agent SEC benchmark uses no small models and no public data; three arXiv abstract-level searches for XBRL/FiNER-139 combined with latency/throughput/quantization/distillation returned zero results. Recommended reframing: "first accuracy-cost-latency frontier for fine-tuned 1-3B (and quantized) models on FinTagging, benchmarked against frontier APIs", explicitly citing FinLoRA, FinTagging, FLaME and 2603.22651 as the closest prior work.
