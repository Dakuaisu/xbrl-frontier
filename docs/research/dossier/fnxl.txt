### task_summary
Task: assign a US-GAAP XBRL tag to each numeral span in a sentence from SEC 10-K text, from an extreme label set of 2,794 tags. Input = sentence (text only; the paper states "we focus only on annotated textual data and discard annotated tables"), output = per-numeral tag, either as BIO sequence labelling or as per-numeral extreme classification. Two benchmark formulations: (a) FiNER-style BERT sequence labelling; (b) pipeline = BERT binary tagger (is this numeral taggable?) + AttentionXML extreme classifier over inputs where other numerals are masked with [MASK]/[NUM]/[SHAPE]. It does NOT cover period, unit, or table cells; it is a concept-labelling benchmark on narrative text only. [VERIFIED from PDF body]

### data
Source: "publicly available annual 10-K reports from 2019-2021 for 2,339 companies"; 160K annotated sentences found, filtered to >=50 chars, numeric-only annotations, US-GAAP labels only, some manual cleaning. Company-disjoint splits (~78:9:13), Table 1: Train 62,782 sentences / 798 companies / 111,493 data points / 2,692 labels; Dev 6,823 / 756 / 13,191 / 1,273; Test 9,483 / 794 / 18,238 / 1,374; Total 79,088 / 840 / 142,922 / 2,794 (as printed). Avg sentence 37.83 tokens (sd 20.37, max 590). Dev/test contain 40 and 69 labels unseen in train (76 and 119 numerals). Heavy tail: top-150 labels (>200 pts each) = 58.79% of data points; 1,856 labels with <20 pts = 8.34%. 100 labels overlap with FiNER-139. ### small_models_evaluated
In the FNXL paper: BERT-base (~110M) and SEC-BERT-base / SEC-num / SEC-shape (~110M) as FiNER sequence labellers; a BERT-based binary tagger plus AttentionXML (BiLSTM + label-tree attention, ~112M) as the XC pipeline. No LLMs, no frontier APIs. Follow-ons on FNXL: FLAN-T5-Large 780M (+LoRA/prefix), T5-Base 220M, T5-Large 780M, GalaXC 41M, Label-Semantics 219M (FLAN-FinXC); Llama 3.1 8B (+LoRA/QLoRA/DoRA/rsLoRA) in FinLoRA; 7B-class open models in FinBen (all 0.00 on FNXL); Qwen2 7B / Llama 3.1 8B in Open FinLLM Leaderboard. No published result for a 1-3B model on FNXL was found.

### relevance_to_idea
FNXL is correctly characterized by the project: it is a text-only, concept-tag-only (no period/unit/table) XBRL benchmark whose paper reports accuracy (Macro/Micro-F1, Hits@k) and training wall-clock on a P100, but NO inference latency, throughput, or serving $. The narrow claim "FNXL reports accuracy but not serving cost or latency" is VERIFIED. The broader claim "nobody has benchmarked small or distilled models on FinTagging/FNXL/FiNER with cost or latency" must be softened because of FinLoRA (Wang et al., arXiv 2505.19819, 2025; https://github.com/Open-Finance-Lab/FinLoRA): it evaluates Llama 3.1 8B (8-bit LoRA, 4-bit QLoRA, DoRA, rsLoRA) against GPT-4o/DeepSeek-V3/Gemini on FNXL and FiNER-139 subsets, reports fine-tuning cost in USD (Table 5: "LoRA 14.9h 4 x A5000 $15.50; QLoRA 14.1h 4 x A5000 $14.66; DoRA 15.9h 4 x A5000 $16.54; rsLoRA 14.5h 4 x A5000 $15.11; Gemini 2.0 FL 8.8h - $162.02; GPT-4o-mini - - $312.00", at "$0.26 per GPU hour"), and plots per-task inference latency (Figure 3: "Average inference time of LoRA fine-tuned Llama 3.1 8B and LoRA fine-tuned Gemini 2.0 FL across tasks", y-axis "Inference Time (seconds)", tasks include FiNER and FNXL), stating "Gemini API generally exhibits lower inference latency and is less sensitive to increasing prompt lengths than local Llama 3.1 8B Instruct inference". Gaps FinLoRA leaves that the project can still own: (1) no model below 8B; (2) no per-fact/per-document serving $ or throughput (only seconds-per-query bars plus training cost); (3) no vLLM or quantized-serving variants (4-bit appears only as QLoRA training); (4) FNXL is evaluated on a 247-item LLM subset, not the 18,238-numeral test set; (5) nothing on FinTagging. Also relevant: FLAN-FinXC (NAACL 2024) reports "training time: 56 minutes/epoch, inference time: 2 minutes/sample" on "Tesla V100 32GB GPUs" for FLAN-T5-Large+LoRA, i.e. a crude latency number for a 780M model on FNXL, but no cost/throughput; XBRLTagRec (Mar 2026) mentions "computational and API costs" only qualitatively; FinBen reports only an aggregate "$51,000" / "600 hours" on 16 A100s for the whole benchmark; the FiNER-139 paper reports only "4 nvidia v100 gpus"; FinTagging reports no cost/latency at all. Recommendation: cite FNXL as the canonical extreme-label text benchmark and reuse its company-disjoint split and Macro-F1/Hits@k protocol; cite FLAN-FinXC and XBRLTagRec as current FNXL SOTA; position explicitly against FinLoRA (e.g., "first sub-8B, per-1k-facts $, vLLM-served accuracy-cost-latency frontier on FinTagging, with FNXL/FiNER as secondary"). Reuse caveat: the HF card says "Research-only use" and "Direct commercial use without explicit permission", and the release is undocumented (one open "Rest of the Data" issue), so verify the CSV contents match Table 1 before depending on it; the FinBen/FinLoRA 318/247-item FNXL subsets are not comparable to the full test set.
