### task_summary
Text-only XBRL tag assignment for a given numeral in a 10-K sentence (financial numeral labeling / extreme classification over 2,794 US-GAAP tags), NOT full fact extraction (no value/period/unit extraction; the numeral and sentence are given). Pipeline: (A) FLAN-T5-Large fine-tuned with LoRA generates a semantic 'tag document' from instruction prompt + sentence + question about the target numeral; (B) Sentence-T5-XXL embeds the generated doc and all ground-truth tag docs, retrieves Top-10 by cosine similarity; (C) ChatGPT-3.5-Turbo-1106 zero-shot re-ranks: Top-10 split into 2 groups of 5 (order-preserving), each group's top pick is voted, repeated for up to 13 rounds, argmax of votes is the prediction. Tables are not handled. [VERIFIED from HTML body]

### models_and_results

Encoder: Sentence-T5-XXL. Re-ranker: ChatGPT-3.5-Turbo-1106 (openai==1.66.3 in requirements). Metrics: Hits@1, Macro-P, Macro-R, Macro-F1 on the partial FNXL. ### relevance_to_idea
SUPPORTS the narrow novelty claim for this paper: XBRLTagRec reports accuracy only (Hits@1/Macro-F1 on a text-only FNXL subset), names a V100 but gives zero latency/throughput/cost numbers, and its production path depends on 4-26 GPT-3.5 API calls per numeral, which it never prices. It does not touch FinTagging, tables, or 1-3B decoder models, so it is not a direct competitor to a 1-3B extraction model served with vLLM. CITE it as the current FNXL text-tagging SOTA (Hits@1 0.8618 / M-F1 0.5050 on the public subset) and as an example of accuracy-only reporting with an unpriced API-reranker dependency. CAUTIONS for the broader claim 'nobody reports cost/latency': (a) its predecessor FLAN-FinXC (NAACL 2024, arXiv 2405.06671) DOES print a coarse figure -- 'with an lr of 5e-4 with LoRA (training time: 56 minutes/epoch, inference time: 2 minutes/sample)' on Tesla V100 32GB, plus a Table 8 of training time per epoch for baselines (FiNER 12h, Label Semantics 9h, GalaXC 1.2h, AttentionXML 4h, FLAN-T5-Large w/ LoRA 56 min) [VERIFIED]; (b) FinTagging itself (arXiv 2505.20650) already benchmarks sub-8B models -- Qwen3-0.6B, Qwen3-1.7B, Llama-3.2-3B-Instruct, Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct, Fino1-8B -- for accuracy (e.g. Llama-3.2-3B-Instruct FinCL accuracy 0.0415; Qwen3-1.7B 0.0735; Qwen3-0.6B 0.0414; DeepSeek-V3 best at 0.1889) and gives only an aggregate budget: 'Models are evaluated with the LM Evaluation Harness on a 4 x H100 cluster and via APIs ... Total cost is approximately 500 GPU-hours and $1,500 in API usage.' [VERIFIED]. So the defensible framing is: no XBRL-tagging paper reports per-query serving cost, latency, or throughput, nor fine-tuned/quantized small-model variants on FinTagging; FinTagging's zero-shot small-model numbers are the baseline the project must beat, and FLAN-FinXC's '2 minutes/sample' is the only prior timing figure to acknowledge. REUSE: public FNXL subset (Google Drive via FLAN-FinXC repo; HF mirrors TheFinAI/flare-fnxl, soummyaah-space/FNXL) and XBRLTagRec's LoRA recipe/metrics for an FNXL side-experiment. AVOID: claiming the full FNXL is available (it is not), or comparing against FLAN-FinXC's self-reported 66.23 Macro-F1 without noting the subset discrepancy.
