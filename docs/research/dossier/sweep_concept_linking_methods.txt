### verdict_on_novelty
From the concept-linking angle the claim is DENTED, not dead. (1) The exact sub-claim 'nobody reports cost for small models on FNXL/FiNER' is false: FLaME (Findings ACL 2025, arXiv 2506.15846) tabulates per-dataset USD cost including FNXL for Llama 3 8B ($0.06), Mistral 7B ($0.06), Gemma 2 9B ($0.08) alongside GPT-4o ($0.96) and DeepSeek R1 ($11.21) -- but zero-shot, near-zero F1, API-priced, no latency ('we do not report exhaustive speed benchmarks'). (2) FinLoRA (arXiv 2505.19819) fine-tunes a quantized Llama-3.1-8B on FiNER-139/FNXL (74.10 / 23.57 acc vs GPT-4o 72.29 / 42.41), reports fine-tuning cost ($14.66-$16.54) and an inference-time figure vs the Gemini API -- the closest prior, but 8B not 1-3B, no per-query serving cost, latency not per task, not FinTagging. (3) FinTagging itself already scores Qwen3-0.6B/1.7B and Llama-3.2-3B on FinCL (0.0414 / 0.0735 / 0.0415) zero-shot, so 'nobody benchmarked small models on FinTagging' must be narrowed to 'no fine-tuned/distilled small model'; it reports only an aggregate '500 GPU-hours and $1,500'. (4) FHS (arXiv 2608.06614, Aug 2026) is the best published FinCL linker (Acc 0.255, R@1 0.185 on the 2,509-fact split with Qwen3-32B, BM25 K=200 + structured decoding) and does report calls/retrievals per fact and wall-clock, but only on CodiEsp and only at 32B. (5) XBRLTagRec (IJCNN 2026) and FLAN-FinXC (NAACL 2024) already do small-generator + retrieval (+LLM rerank) linking on FNXL (Hits@1 0.8618 / 0.8998, Macro-F1 0.505 / 0.662) with no cost and at most a stray '2 minutes/sample' timing. (6) Unpublished signals: lm2445's July-2026 FinTagging SFT/grounding datasets and the Aug-2026 sec-xbrl-hard-negatives retriever triplets show others are moving toward small-model FinCL. What remains genuinely unoccupied: a 1-3B fine-tuned (or distilled) model evaluated on FinTagging FinNI+FinCL over the full 17.7k-concept taxonomy with a measured accuracy / $-per-filing / p50-p95 latency frontier under vLLM and quantization, compared head-to-head with frontier APIs on the same split. Numbers to beat: FinCL acc 0.255 (FHS, 32B, 2,509-fact split) or 0.1889 (DeepSeek-V3, full FinCL-eval); retrieval ceiling Acc@200 0.3274 (text-embedding-3-small) vs FHS Recall@200 0.705; FNXL Hits@1 0.8618 / Macro-F1 0.505 (XBRLTagRec); FiNER-139 Macro-F1 94.74 (FLAN-FinXC) and 74.10 acc for FinLoRA's 8B.
