### verdict_on_novelty
From this angle the claim is DENTED, not dead, and must be reworded. (a) 'Nobody reports cost or latency for small models on FiNER/FNXL' is literally false: FinLoRA (arXiv 2505.19819, VERIFIED) fine-tunes Llama 3.1 8B with LoRA/QLoRA on FiNER-139 and FNXL and reports fine-tuning dollar cost ('$14.66 to $16.54' on 4xA5000) plus 'Average inference time of LoRA fine-tuned Llama 3.1 8B and LoRA fine-tuned Gemini 2.0 FL across tasks' (Figure 3) and a qualitative API-vs-local latency comparison; FLAN-FinXC (arXiv 2405.06671, VERIFIED) reports 'inference time: 2 minutes/sample' for a 780M FLAN-T5 on FNXL. Neither gives inference $ cost, sub-8B generative models, quantized/vLLM serving, or an accuracy-cost-latency frontier against frontier APIs. (b) On FinTagging specifically the claim is INTACT: the FinTagging paper (VERIFIED) has 'Zero mentions of inference cost, latency, throughput, or runtime', evaluates 0.5B-8B models zero-shot only, and no fetched source benchmarks any fine-tuned or distilled model on FinTagging with cost or latency. (c) The broader 'prior work on SEC-filing numeric extraction reports accuracy but not cost/latency' is DENTED by Kulkarni & Kulkarni 2026 (arXiv 2603.22651, VERIFIED: $0.148-$0.430/doc and 21-74s p50 on 10,000 10-K/10-Q/8-K with XBRL-derived labels, but only >=70B/frontier models, no fine-tuning, no public release) and by EarningsBench (VERIFIED: $0.249 and 13-148s per earnings release, frontier only, private). (d) The generic thesis 'fine-tuned small open models match frontier APIs at 10-100x lower cost and lower latency' is already well established and reproducible: distil labs (0.6B-8B Qwen3 on vLLM, $3 vs $6,241 per 1M requests, p50/p95/p99 latency, public repo), Manulife 'Beyond Accuracy' (fine-tuned SmolVLM-500M/2.2B and Qwen2.5-VL-3B/7B vs GPT-5/Claude on bank checks with $/doc and p95 latency, public code), Olava (self-hosted fine-tuned SLM vs five frontier APIs on SEC EDGAR contracts, $0.018 vs $0.149-$0.456/doc, 131s vs 93-194s), Mastercard (270M-8B LoRA throughput frontier on financial transaction extraction), TensorZero, LoRA Land, plus Nanonets/Ertas/Fireworks/OpenPipe marketing. So a headline of 'small fine-tuned models are cheaper than GPT-class APIs for financial extraction' will read as generic. The defensible, still-open contribution is narrower and should be stated as: the first public, reproducible accuracy/cost/latency frontier for 1-3B fine-tuned models (with quantized vLLM serving curves) on XBRL-style fact extraction from real SEC 10-K/10-Q tables and text, evaluated on FinTagging (optionally FNXL/FiNER) against frontier APIs - positioned explicitly relative to FinLoRA (8B, fine-tuning cost only, no serving frontier), Kulkarni 2026 (frontier/70B, no fine-tuning, private), and Manulife/Olava (different document types).
