### verdict_on_novelty
From this angle the claim is DENTED, not dead, and should be reworded. (1) "Nobody has benchmarked small or distilled models on FinTagging" is false as literally stated: FinTagging v5 itself reports Qwen3-0.6B/1.7B, Llama-3.2-3B and DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen-32B zero-shot plus fine-tuned BERT-large/FinBERT/SECBERT (https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.20650); FinAI Contest 2025 Task 2 @ IEEE CSCloud evaluated <=8B fine-tuned entries on FinNI (200) / FinCL (500) subsets ("The model size should not exceed 8B parameters", https://github.com/Open-Finance-Lab/FinAI_Contest_2025/tree/main/Task_2_FinGPT_Agents_Real_Life), and public 8-bit/4-bit Llama-3.1-8B FinNI/FinCL LoRA adapters exist (https://huggingface.co/LbbbbbY/FinAI_Contest_FinGPT). However, no published accuracy numbers for a FINE-TUNED 1-3B model on FinTagging were found anywhere. (2) "Prior work reports accuracy but NOT serving cost or latency" is partially false on FiNER/FNXL: FinLoRA (https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.19819) reports fine-tuning cost ("$14.66 to $16.54"), 4-bit memory (4.02 GB), and an inference-time comparison (LoRA Llama-3.1-8B local vs Gemini 2.0 FL API, Fig. 3) on FiNER-139/FNXL/XBRL extraction; on FinTagging, FHS (https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.06614) reports LLM-call counts per fact and wall-clock hours (Qwen3-32B, single B200, CodiEsp split only), and FinTagging itself gives only an aggregate "500 GPU-hours and $1,500 in API usage" budget; FinReporting (https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.05966) reports per-model API $ for an XBRL-adjacent pipeline. (3) What remains INTACT: no work found that (a) fine-tunes a 1-3B open model on FinTagging FinNI+FinCL, (b) serves it via vLLM in quantized variants, and (c) reports a per-model accuracy vs $/filing vs latency/throughput frontier against frontier APIs on FinTagging; no FinTagging result anywhere carries serving cost or throughput numbers, and no shared task (FinNLP/FinEval 2026, CLEF FinMMEval 2026, FinRL/SecureFinAI 2025) scores cost or latency. The defensible claim is the narrower one: "no published accuracy-cost-latency frontier for fine-tuned small (1-3B) models on FinTagging (or FiNER/FNXL), and no serving-cost or throughput numbers for any FinTagging result"; FinLoRA must be cited and differentiated explicitly (8B, no FinTagging, latency only as a single figure vs one API, no serving $ or throughput).
