(.00066) is fully read (open PDF in the team's GitHub repo): it never touches XBRL/FinNI/FinCL; it does report 4-bit Llama-2-7B, Colab A100-40GB, and 47.9 s average processing time, but only for 30 CFA-style questions. (.00061 FinVerse) is unverifiable without IEEE access; its abstract/keywords name no efficiency metric. The broader clause 'nobody benchmarked ... FNXL/FiNER with cost or latency' must be narrowed: FinLoRA (arXiv 2505.19819) reports average inference time (Figure 3) and fine-tuning cost for LoRA-tuned Llama-3.1-8B on FiNER-139/FNXL/XBRL Term, albeit no sub-8B model, no vLLM/quantized serving comparison, and no $-per-fact frontier; so qualify as 'no sub-8B / no serving-cost-vs-API-frontier'.
