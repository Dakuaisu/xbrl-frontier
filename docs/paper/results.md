# Results

## Numeric fact extraction on the development split

The final Qwen3-1.7B adapter achieved **91.47% micro-F1** on the deduplicated internal development split, compared with 90.24% at the end of the first pass over the same 37,721 distinct instances. Five first-pass checkpoints and the final adapter were evaluated on the same split using the numeric identification task (FinNI) described by [Wang et al., *FinTagging: Benchmarking LLMs for Extracting and Structuring Financial Information*, arXiv:2505.20650v5](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.20650v5). The local scorer matches sets of (normalized numeric value, type) pairs within each instance and pools true positives (TP), false positives (FP), and false negatives (FN) before computing precision, recall, and F1. This split is not the public FinNI test set.

**Table 1.** [Six-row development learning curve](./learning-curve.md): pooled micro-F1 overall and for table and text contexts, raw and deduplicated, with parse failures and cut-offs. Deduplicated results are primary. Unparseable responses count as empty predictions.

Recall peaked at the 20,000-instance checkpoint. Compared with that checkpoint, the final adapter's higher F1 came from higher precision: false positives fell from 2,710 to 1,551 on the deduplicated split, while false negatives rose from 290 to 488. Retaining only the first occurrence of each exact context–answer pair removed 13 evaluation rows and did not change the ordering of the six overall F1 scores. The table reports cut-offs and parse failures at every checkpoint.

**Figure 1.** [Development-set micro-F1 by checkpoint](./checkpoint-f1.svg), overall and by context type. Lines connect five first-pass nested snapshots only. The final adapter is shown separately at the same 37,721-instance x-value as the end-of-first-pass checkpoint: it was saved at step 4,716 after **two total epochs** (2,358 steps per epoch); 1,067 of the 38,788 available instances exceeded the 4,096-token limit and were skipped. The paired endpoints show a descriptive within-run change with no new distinct instances; they are not independent training-size experiments. The vertical axes are truncated to 0.70–0.95.

**Paired uncertainty.** The final checkpoint improved on the 10,000-instance checkpoint by **+4.87 F1 points (95% CI: +3.25 to +7.09)** on the deduplicated split. We computed the percentile interval from 1,000 paired bootstrap resamples of the 17 companies (SEC Central Index Keys, or CIKs). Each replicate drew the same companies with replacement for both checkpoints, pooled their TP, FP, and FN counts, and recomputed the difference in micro-F1. The interval reflects variation across the sampled companies for this training seed, not variation across training runs.

**Score artifacts.** The [curve table](./learning-curve.md) identifies all six local-only score and prediction reports by SHA-256. The paired interval above is specifically for final versus 10,000 and was computed from those two reports' per-instance counts; no interval is claimed for the same-x comparison.
